Seller Agreement

Last updated: add the date you publish

Draft in plain language. Have a Nigerian lawyer review it and fill in the highlighted parts before you open to the public.

1. Becoming a seller

You must be 18 or older and sell goods you made or have the right to sell. You agree that the details and documents you send are true and belong to you, and that your next of kin and guarantor agreed to be contacted. Admins decide within 2 working days. We may refuse or end an application without having to give every reason, but we will tell you when something is missing.

2. Listing products

Each new product, category and story is checked by admins within 24 to 48 hours before buyers see it. Use real photos of your real product, give honest descriptions and prices, and keep stock accurate. Do not list illegal, fake or copied goods.

3. Orders and delivery

When an order is confirmed, prepare it quickly. Our delivery targets are under 24 hours in Bayelsa, within 48 hours in nearby states and 3 to 5 days elsewhere. Pack it well.

4. Fees and payouts

The platform keeps a percentage of each sale (shown in your Earnings tab, currently set by the super admin). Your money is released a set number of days after the buyer receives the order, unless the buyer reports a problem. You then request a payout, and we pay the bank account you gave us. The smallest payout is ₦1,000. If a refund is approved for a sale already paid out to you, we may take it from later earnings.

5. Problems and refunds

If a buyer reports a problem, an admin reviews it and may approve a refund. We may hold the related money until it is decided.

6. Your data

We store your documents and details encrypted and use them only as described in our Privacy Policy.

7. Ending

We may suspend or remove a seller who breaks these rules, sells fake goods, or harms buyers. You may close your seller account at any time after your open orders are completed.

8. Funding and community

Loan and grant requests are only passed on to a partner. We do not promise any money or any result. The business community group is for learning and respect; we may remove members who misuse it.
