What we collect

  • Everyone: name, email, phone number, delivery addresses, orders and messages.
  • Sellers, to verify the business: business name and address, CAC number or NIN, a copy of a valid ID, a passport photograph, the NIN slip or CAC certificate, the bank account for payouts, and the names, phone numbers and addresses of a next of kin and a guarantor.
  • Photos and voice you choose to use: a photo you send for photo search is used once to find products and is not kept. Voice search uses your phone's own speech recognition.

Why we use it

To run your account, deliver orders, confirm payments, pay sellers, check that sellers are real, prevent fraud, and answer your problems. We do not sell your data.

How we protect it

  • Passwords are stored scrambled and cannot be read, even by us.
  • NIN, bank account numbers and uploaded documents are encrypted. Only admins can open them, and each time is recorded in a log.
  • After a seller is approved we delete the full NIN and keep only the last 4 digits.
  • Documents and sensitive details of rejected applicants are deleted after 30 days.

Who sees it

Our admins, and the services we use to run the app: hosting and database, email, and AI tools that help admins read documents and receipts and help with search and the assistant. If you give us a guarantor or next of kin, you confirm they agreed to be contacted.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, and to stop receiving messages. Some records, such as orders and payments, we must keep for a time. Write to your privacy email. If you are unhappy, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Keeping it

We keep account and order records while your account is open and for as long as the law or a dispute requires. Add your exact retention periods.
